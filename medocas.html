Privacy Policy for MEDOCAS TUNNEL VPN

Effective Date: Aug 14, 2026

MEDOCAS TUNNEL VPN ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of our users. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and under what circumstances we may disclose it when you use our VPN application.

Information We Collect

To ensure proper functioning, connection security, and optimized performance of our VPN service, we may collect the following limited information:

  • Device ID & Advertising ID: We collect unique device identifiers and Google Advertising ID (AD_ID) to verify authorization, manage push notifications, maintain connection security, and display advertisements.
  • Device Information: We collect device hardware model, operating system version, and performance diagnostics to optimize app compatibility and troubleshoot app crashes.

Information We Do NOT Collect (No-Logs Policy)

Your online privacy is our priority. We strictly adhere to a No-Logs Policy. We do NOT collect, monitor, store, or log:

  • Your browsing history or websites visited.
  • IP addresses assigned during your VPN session.
  • Connection timestamps or session duration linked to personal identities.
  • Traffic destinations, payload data, or content of your data transfers.
  • Your DNS queries while connected to our VPN servers.

Third-Party Services & Advertising

Our app integrates third-party services to deliver ads, analytics, and notification services. These third parties may process device information according to their respective privacy policies:

  • AdMob & Google AdServices: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device IDs, Advertising IDs, coarse location, and app interaction data to serve contextual or personalized ads.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): We use Firebase services to deliver push notifications and service updates. FCM processes basic device tokens to ensure notification delivery.

Use of Information

We use the collected information solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Service Maintenance: Providing, maintaining, and improving the VPN connection quality.
  • Security & Prevention: Identifying and preventing unauthorized access, abuse, or malicious activity on our network.
  • Ad Delivery & Analytics: Displaying non-intrusive ads and analyzing app stability (crash logs).

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to outside parties. Information is shared only with trusted third-party SDKs (such as Google AdMob) strictly for advertising and app functionalities as described above, or if required by law to comply with a legal obligation.

Security

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (such as OpenVPN / V2Ray protocols) to protect your data during transit. While we take commercially reasonable measures to protect your information, no transmission method over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

User Choices & Data Retention

You can stop all data collection by uninstalling the MEDOCAS TUNNEL VPN app from your device at any time.
